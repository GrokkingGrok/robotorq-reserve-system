# The RoboTorq Concept Stack

**Version 1.0**

A minimal, dependency-ordered ladder of concepts.

---

## Tier 0: Physical Reality (Root Layer)

**Axioms:**
1. Robot power rating is measurable in Joules / Watt-hours and tokens in tokens/sec (Bonded, signed tokens generated by `Digger` that tracks robots, not neural nets)
2. Robotic labor priced based on capacity for energy consumption and max token throughput regardless of efficiency of use
3. Energy consumption x tokens can be tracked, hashed, signed, and timestamped

> **Foundation**: Nothing above this tier this works unless these three statements are true.

---

## Tier 1: Economic Primitive

**Core Definition:**
- A currency unit can be defined as a **fixed amount of physical work**
  - *Example*: `1 RoboTorq = 1 kWh normalized × 1 hour (time-gated)`

**Economic Order:**
- Work performed **first** → currency issued **second**
  - *Prevents speculation-based inflation*

> **Status**: At this tier, RoboTorq is not yet a system—it's just a definition.

---

## Tier 2: Cryptographic Representation

**Data Structures:**
- **Ore**: Every tiny chunk of work = one signed JouleTorqOre unit
- **Aggregation**: Ore can be batched into larger artifacts (Ingots of 3600 Ore units) with Merkle proofs
- **Certificates**: Represent a fixed batch of 1000 ingots and act as reserves

**Key Question Answered:**
> *"How do you represent physical labor as digital objects that can't be forged?"*

---

## Tier 3: System Dynamics

**Economic Rules:**
1. **Backing**: Certificates back digital RoboTorq 1:1
2. **Issuance Control**: Vault verifies that no more currency is issued than certificates allow
3. **Circulation**: Demurrage on idle balances funds new robotic labor

**Closed-Loop Economy:**
> This tier defines the complete economic system.

**Key Invariant:**
```
The system cannot mint more RoboTorq unless more robotic labor occurs.
```

---

## Tier 4: System Architecture (Mechanics)

**Pipeline:**
```
Robots → Digger → Refinery → Mint → Vault → Wallet 
```

**Security Model:**
- Each service **signs** what it sees
- Each service **validates** what it receives
- No global blockchain—just local proofs passed forward

> **Implementation**: This is the "machine that makes and moves the money."

---

## Tier 5: Monetary Behavior (User-Facing)

**User Experience:**
- Users earn **dividends** from system circulation
- **Bearer Bonds**: Physical, certificate-backed, demurrage-free
- **UBD (Universal Basic Dividend)**: Flows algorithmically from certificates through wallets

> **Human Layer**: This is "what the system feels like to a human."

---

## Tier 6: Governance & Expansion

**System Controls:**
- **Vault**: Enforces contract solvency (no unfunded work)
- **BidNet**: Allows investment in new robotic labor projects
- **Decentralization**: Community nodes can run the system with minimal hardware

> **Future-Facing**: This tier is designed for growth, but depends cleanly on previous tiers.

---

## Tier 7: Optional Simulations & Optimization

**Research Layer:**
- Macroscale economics can be **simulated** by scaling time constants
- Robotic labor markets can be **tested safely** under load
- Academic exploration of system dynamics

> **Placement**: This is where the "academic simulator" sits—completely downstream from operational system.

---

## Summary

Each tier depends **only** on the tiers below it. No circular dependencies. No speculative layers required for core functionality.

**Read from bottom to top** to understand dependencies.  
**Read from top to bottom** to understand capabilities.
