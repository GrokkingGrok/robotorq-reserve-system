## b2b (bot2builder): The b2b Bot Work Share Network

The goal of v0 of the RoboTorq Economy is to create a p2p network connecting bot owners/maintainers with businesses who want robotic labor, but do not want the hassle of ownership and fleet maintenance.

The network will utilize a compact version of the bond network conceptualized by "The RoboTorq Paper: Watts > Wall Street".

An open-source network will coordinate funding, bidding, and execution of b2b smart contracts for robotic labor. 

So long as a builder has a buyer or distributor lined up before bidding, they can rely on their bot providers

Oracle nodes on the network accept data for verification of work purposes (production line cameras, distributor purchase orders)

RoboTorq is minted based on the smart contracts, and the minted money is distributed to all members of the network for the purpose of purchasing the goods made.

However, this does not occur until proof-of-work verifications (PoWv) needed from the oracle nodes for each mint request have been processed.

Members of the network have only one job to continue receiving their constant stream of RoboTorq dividends: ensure that PoWv happen on a timely basis.
