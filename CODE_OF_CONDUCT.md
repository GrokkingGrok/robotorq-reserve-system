# Code of Conduct

We adopt a modified Contributor Covenant to foster an open, welcoming, and high‑integrity community focused on physics‑anchored economic systems.

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in RoboTorq community a harassment‑free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio‑economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards
Positive behaviors:
- Demonstrating empathy and respect for differing viewpoints
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing critiques on code, architecture, and testability—not people
- Recognizing time constraints; keeping discussions concise
- Prioritizing user safety, security, and verifiability

Unacceptable behaviors:
- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Discriminatory jokes or slurs
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Sustained disruption of discussions or PR reviews

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Maintainers review reported incidents, may request clarifications, and will act to preserve community health.

## Scope
Applies to all project spaces—GitHub issues, PRs, discussions, docs, code reviews—and external channels representing the project.

## Reporting
Report incidents privately:
- Security or conduct: email `security@robotorq.example` (placeholder; update with real address)
- Or open a confidential GitHub issue labeled `private` (do not disclose sensitive details publicly)

Include: date/time, participants (GitHub handles), context, and any supporting links. We will acknowledge receipt within 72 hours.

## Enforcement
Responses may include:
- Clarification and coaching
- Removal of content
- Temporary or permanent account/project bans
- Escalation to platform administrators (GitHub Trust & Safety)

## Appeal
If you believe an action was taken in error, request a review via the reporting email referencing the original incident ID.

This Code will evolve. Propose amendments via a `docs: update code of conduct` PR referencing rationale and potential impacts.
